## What Is a Specimen Passport?

A cryptographic certificate for each physical constant, similar to:
- Passport for person (identity + verification)
- Certificate for precious metal (purity + weight)
- Certificate of authenticity (origin + verification)

**Passport Contents:**
- ✅ Constant identity (name, symbol, unit)
- ✅ WAD18 value (exact integer)
- ✅ Uncertainty (measurement precision)
- ✅ Derivation (fundamental or derived)
- ✅ Hash verification (SHA-256)
- ✅ Source certification (NIST/CODATA)
- ✅ Timestamp (ISO-8601)
- ✅ Acceptance status (approved/verified)

## EXPIRATION & RENEWAL

Specimen passports expire when:
- ✅ New CODATA release occurs (every 4 years)
- ✅ Better measurement published (peer-reviewed)
- ✅ SI redefinition occurs

Action on expiration:
1. Obtain new passport from updated CODATA
2. Compare with previous version
3. Update hash if value changed
4. Reissue with new timestamp
5. Verify all relationships still hold
